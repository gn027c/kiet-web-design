Featured Trails and Waterways

From the gentle ripples of the Argo Cascades to the rugged paths of northern state forests, explore three of our most highly recommended parks.

Gallup Park & River Trails

Spanning over 69 acres along the Huron River, Gallup Park features paved and natural trails, pedestrian bridges, open picnic shelters, and dedicated canoe and kayak liveries.

Explore Gallup Park

Bird Hills Nature Area

The largest park within Ann Arbor city limits, Bird Hills offers 161 acres of uninterrupted forest, rolling hills, and shaded nature paths popular with hikers, trail runners, and bird watchers.

Explore Bird Hills

Argo Cascades & Livery

Constructed with a series of bypass pools and gentle rapids, the Argo Cascades provide a safe, exciting whitewater experience for tubes, kayaks, and stand-up paddleboards.

Explore Argo Cascades
